Hi, I'm Yang! I'm a master's student in physics at Heidelberg University, working in the Data Science group at the Max Planck Institute for Astronomy (MPIA) with Dr. Ivelina Momcheva and Dr. Morgan Fouesneau.

My research sits at the intersection of **observational galaxy evolution** and **machine learning**. I work with space-based imaging and slitless (grism) spectroscopy from **JWST** and **HST** to understand how galaxies at cosmic noon and beyond build up their stars and dust.

**Current work.** For my master's thesis I am building a multi-modal, self-supervised foundation model that fuses sparse, multi-survey JWST data — NIRISS/NIRCam imaging and low-resolution PRISM spectra — into a shared representation via a CLIP-style objective, enabling population-level inference of galaxy properties such as stellar mass.

**Previously.** In my bachelor thesis I measured spatially resolved Balmer decrements of individual galaxies at 0.6 < z < 1.6 from HST/CLEAR and JWST/OutThere grism data, developing a PSF-matching technique for emission-line maps and producing the first r<sub>eff</sub>-normalized Balmer decrement profiles at z > 1.

#### Research interests

- **Science** — (high-z) galaxy evolution · dust attenuation and star formation
- **Data** — space-based extragalactic surveys · wide-field imaging · (grism) spectroscopy
- **Methods** — emission-line diagnostics · spatially resolved analysis · self-supervised machine learning

Outside of research, I play guitar, do (astro)photography, and dive. Feel free to reach out by email!
